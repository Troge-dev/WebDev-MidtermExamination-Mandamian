About InstaFace

InstaFace is a lightweight, privacy-focused social media prototype developed by Rogelio Q. Mandamian III (BSDS-3A) for the Web Development Midterm Examination. It blends the feed and posting experience of Facebook and Instagram with a strict client-side privacy architecture.

“I think, therefore I am.” (Cogito, ergo sum)

— René Descartes, 1637

If thinking is what affirms our existence, what is left of us when we surrender it to machines? InstaFace gives users a space to connect without AI models scraping their personal thoughts or collecting telemetry.

Our Mission

To build a privacy-first social platform where users can share ideas freely without worries of AI data mining, tracking cookies, or corporate surveillance.

Our Vision

To prove that clean front-end web applications can deliver realistic user experiences using only HTML, CSS Flexbox, and Vanilla JavaScript with no external backend or database.

Four Core Pillars

  1. Human Thinking: Encouraging original human ideas rather than AI-generated content.
  2. Client Isolation: All data stays stored inside the user's browser.
  3. Code Simplicity: Clean, valid code that can be opened and edited in Windows Notepad.
  4. Free Expression: Connect with peers without algorithmic manipulation.
